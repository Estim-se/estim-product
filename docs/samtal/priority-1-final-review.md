# Priority 1 final independent safety review

2026-11-26; reviewed available main `16e6ee3` and the unchanged accepted proposal. No live inspection or activation.

An independent read-only reviewer inspected the SQL, its design/results and existing Samtal/shared dispatch source. Its conclusions were checked with independent PostgreSQL sessions and test-only application flows. The accepted SQL remains unchanged; one separately labelled candidate amendment was prepared, not merged into the accepted draft.

## Lock order and scalability

Accepted order: batch advisory (batch RPCs), company advisory, company row, call, job, ledger/receipt. Single-company APIs do not use the global batch lock and independent companies progressed concurrently in a local test. Company rows precede unchanged `estim_receive`, which also locks that row.

Batch candidates are ordered by due time, not globally by company. Return limits (50/500) bound result count, **not candidate scans or wait times**. A global batch may retain multiple tenant locks until commit. The installation's five-second `SET LOCAL lock_timeout` does not configure future RPCs. Local harness sessions explicitly use 5s lock and 10s statement timeouts; these are not production settings.

No benchmark in this work measures live throughput, STT/AI, HTTP dispatch, object storage or large shared customer catalogs. The existing 5,000-customer shortlist should be reviewed for large organizations; broad exact phone resolution in shared ingestion remains unchanged.

## Administration and GDPR

The existing administration handler supports one-company activation/deactivation and membership operations; new restrictive FKs do not prevent those updates. Its shared code was not modified. Old Samtal service writers intentionally become incompatible after the proposed revoke; they must be drained before coordinated activation.

Terminalizing raw call content is **not complete GDPR erasure** of tenant/call/request history. Indefinite UUID/path/receipt retention can remain linkable to customer records. Before claiming erasure support, approve: an authenticated Samtal retirement gate; stop late writers; revoke/expire upload authority; terminalize scoped content; verify external deletion and unresolved attempts; handle each shared record under its separate legal retention basis; safely retire receipt/ledger rows; preserve a minimal approved replay-denial mechanism without recreating erased calls. No such cross-module action is authorized by this preparation.

## Verdict

Local integration preparation is useful and the tested terminal guards remain intact. **Production activation is still blocked** by dispatch recovery, erasure/ledger lifecycle, cleanup capacity, remaining lease/end-check coverage and approved uncertainty/replay policy. The token amendment is only one candidate fix. Passing reproduction tests identify existing bad behavior; they do not assert those gaps are solved.
